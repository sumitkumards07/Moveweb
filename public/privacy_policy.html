Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Move ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our ride-matching mobile application and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you register or use our Service, we may collect:

  • Full name and email address
  • Phone number
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Payment information (processed by third-party payment processors; we do not store full card numbers)
  • Driver-specific information: government-issued ID, vehicle details, and insurance documents

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Service, we automatically collect:

  • Precise GPS location data (foreground and, if permitted, background) to enable ride matching and navigation
  • Device identifiers (device ID, advertising ID, operating system version)
  • App usage data (session duration, features accessed, crash reports)
  • IP address and network information

1.3 How We Collect This Data

We collect data through: (a) information you enter directly into the app during registration and use; (b) automatic collection via device sensors, GPS, and Apple APIs when you grant permission; (c) third-party SDKs and services integrated into our app (detailed in Section 4).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the Service, including matching drivers with riders in real time
  • To process payments and prevent fraud
  • To communicate with you via email, SMS, or push notifications about your trips, account, and Service updates
  • To improve and personalize the Service based on usage patterns
  • To comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes
  • For safety and security monitoring of the platform

Data collected for one purpose will not be used for a materially different purpose without your explicit consent, unless required by law.

3. Location Data

We collect and process your location to enable core features of the Service, including real-time ride matching and navigation. Location data is collected only when you have granted permission through your device settings.

You can enable or disable location access at any time in your device Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. Note that disabling location access will prevent you from using core features of the Service.

We do not sell your location data to third parties. Location data shared with drivers is limited to what is necessary to complete your trip.

  • Payment Processing: Payments are processed by [Your Payment Processor, e.g., Stripe]. They receive transaction data necessary to complete payments and fraud checks.
  • Analytics: We use [e.g., Firebase Analytics / Mixpanel] to understand app usage. These services may collect device identifiers and usage statistics. They do not receive your name or contact information.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Your data is stored on servers hosted by [e.g., Amazon Web Services / Google Cloud], which are contractually bound to data security standards.
  • Customer Support: If you contact support, your communications may be processed by [e.g., Zendesk or Intercom].

4.2 Other Disclosures

  • With your consent: We share data with third parties only when you have explicitly agreed.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or government authority.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

All third-party partners are contractually obligated to provide the same or greater level of data protection as stated in this Privacy Policy, consistent with Apple's App Store Guidelines.

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access, Correction, and Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention obligations)

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@move.com or use the in-app "Account Settings > Privacy" section. We will respond within 30 days.

5.2 Revoking Consent

You can revoke your consent to data collection at any time using the following methods:

  • Location access: Go to your device Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Move App, and change the permission to "Never."
  • Push notifications: Go to Settings > Notifications > Move App and disable notifications.
  • Marketing communications: Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or contact support@move.com.
  • Account deletion: You may delete your account entirely through Settings > Account > Delete Account within the app, or by contacting support@move.com. Account deletion will trigger deletion of your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Please note that revoking certain permissions (such as location) will limit or disable core features of the Service.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. Specifically:

  • Account data is retained for the duration of your active account and deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Trip history and transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years to comply with financial and legal obligations.
  • Location data associated with completed trips is anonymized or deleted within 90 days.
  • Analytics data is retained in aggregated, non-identifiable form.

To request deletion of your personal data, follow the steps in Section 5.2 above.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without verified parental consent, we will take steps to delete such data promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data to us, please contact us at support@move.com.

8. Security of Your Personal Data

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest
  • Access controls limiting who within our organization can access your data
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing

No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

9. International Data Transfers

If you are located outside of [your country, e.g., the United States], your information may be transferred to and processed in countries where data protection laws may differ from those in your country. By using the Service, you consent to such transfers. We take steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers, including standard contractual clauses where required.
