Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tirth Travels ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the Tirth Travels (Rider App) and Tirth Travels Driver (Driver App) mobile applications and related services (collectively, the "Service"). Tirth Travels is the developer and data controller for these apps. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you register or use the Service, we may collect:

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Pickup and drop-off details, trip preferences, and support messages
  • Payment information (processed by third-party payment processors; we do not store full card numbers)
  • Driver onboarding documents, including Driving License and Registration Certificate (RC) uploads
  • Vehicle details, permits, and insurance information for drivers

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Service, we automatically collect:

  • Precise GPS location data (foreground for riders and drivers, and background for drivers while online or on trip)
  • Device identifiers (device ID, advertising ID, operating system version)
  • App usage data (session duration, features accessed, crash reports)
  • IP address and network information

1.3 App Permissions

The Service requests the following permissions to operate core features:

  • Camera: Capture profile photos and scan or upload Driving License and Registration Certificate images.
  • Photos/Media (Gallery): Upload existing document images or profile photos.
  • Location: Enable ride matching, live tracking, and navigation.
  • Notifications: Provide trip updates, safety alerts, and account messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the Service, including matching riders with nearby drivers in real time
  • To match riders with vehicle classes such as Urbania, Traveller, and Innova Crysta
  • To verify driver identity, vehicle eligibility, and required onboarding documents
  • To process payments and prevent fraud
  • To communicate with you via email, SMS, or push notifications about trips, account activity, and Service updates
  • To improve and personalize the Service based on usage patterns
  • To comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes
  • For safety and security monitoring of the platform

Data collected for one purpose will not be used for a materially different purpose without your explicit consent, unless required by law.

3. Location Data

We collect and process location data to enable ride matching, live tracking, and navigation. Location data is collected only when you have granted permission through your device settings.

Rider App: We use foreground location to show pickup points, estimate fares, and track your trip.

Driver App: When you are online, we collect background location to match you with nearby riders and vehicle classes (including Urbania, Traveller, and Innova Crysta), dispatch trips, and share real-time progress with riders. Background location collection stops when you go offline.

You can enable or disable location access at any time in your device Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. Disabling location will prevent you from using core features of the Service.

We do not sell your location data to third parties. Location data shared with riders or drivers is limited to what is necessary to complete a trip.

4. Sharing Your Information with Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with the following categories of third parties, each of whom is required to protect your data to the same standard described in this Privacy Policy and to comply with applicable data protection laws:

4.1 Service Providers

  • Mapping and Navigation: We use mapping APIs to provide routing and navigation. These providers may process your location data subject to their privacy policies.
  • Payment Processing: Payments are processed by third-party payment processors that receive transaction data necessary to complete payments and fraud checks.
  • Analytics: We use analytics services to understand app usage. These services may collect device identifiers and usage statistics.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Your data is stored on servers hosted by cloud providers that are contractually bound to data security standards.
  • Customer Support: If you contact support, your communications may be processed by support service providers.

4.2 Other Disclosures

  • With your consent: We share data with third parties only when you have explicitly agreed.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or government authority.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
  • Safety: We may share data to prevent fraud, protect safety, or investigate violations of our Terms of Service.

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access, Correction, and Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention obligations)

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@tirthtravels.com or use the in-app account settings. We will respond within 30 days.

5.2 Revoking Consent

You can revoke your consent to data collection at any time using the following methods:

  • Location access: Go to your device Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Tirth Travels or Tirth Travels Driver, and change the permission to "Never."
  • Push notifications: Go to Settings > Notifications > Tirth Travels or Tirth Travels Driver and disable notifications.
  • Marketing communications: Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or contact support@tirthtravels.com.
  • Account deletion: You may delete your account through Settings > Account > Delete Account within the app, or by contacting support@tirthtravels.com.

Please note that revoking certain permissions (such as location) will limit or disable core features of the Service.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. Specifically:

  • Account data is retained for the duration of your active account and deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Trip history and transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years to comply with financial and legal obligations.
  • Location data associated with completed trips is anonymized or deleted within 90 days.
  • Analytics data is retained in aggregated, non-identifiable form.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without verified parental consent, we will take steps to delete such data promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data to us, please contact us at support@tirthtravels.com.

8. Security of Your Personal Data

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest
  • Access controls limiting who within our organization can access your data
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing

No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy within the app with a new "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an email or push notification for material changes

Continued use of the Service after the effective date of any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
